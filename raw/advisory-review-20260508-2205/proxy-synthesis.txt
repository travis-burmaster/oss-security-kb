# OSS Security KB — Proposed Page: Django (PyPI)

---

## 1. Recommended KB Status Line

**Package:** `django` (PyPI)
**Ecosystem:** Python / PyPI
**Repository:** https://github.com/django/django
**KB Status:** **ADD — High Priority.** Django is one of the most widely depended-upon PyPI packages, has a mature but continuously active vulnerability history (≥275 unique OSV records), and is currently absent from the KB. Omission represents a significant coverage gap.
